The Character of the King

12 Core Values of the
African Reformation

These are not rules to follow. They are a life to live — the character of Christ made visible in every believer, every family, every community. This is what Bring Back the King looks like in daily human life.

Galatians 5:22–23 · Matthew 5:3–12 · Micah 6:8
1

Unconditional Love

Love that does not depend on how you are treated. Love that gives without counting the cost and serves without needing to be thanked.

Live it · Love someone today who has not earned it
2

Radical Honesty

Telling the truth when a lie is easier. Refusing to deceive, manipulate, or wear a mask. The reformation cannot be built on anything but the truth.

Live it · Say what is true, not what is comfortable
3

Loyal Commitment

Standing by your covenant when it is costly. Loyalty to God, to the community, and to the mission — even when everything tempts you to walk away.

Live it · Keep the promise you made when it was easier
4

Deep Humility

Not thinking less of yourself — thinking of yourself less. The reformer who does not lead with humility does not lead with the King's authority.

Live it · Serve today where no one will notice or applaud
5

Self-Sacrifice

The willingness to lay down your comfort, your time, your resources — and your life — for the good of another. This is the King's defining characteristic.

Live it · Give something today that costs you something real
6

Living Righteousness

Doing what is right even when it is not rewarded. Justice, integrity, and moral courage — not as religion but as a daily way of life that reflects the King's nature.

Live it · Do the right thing in one hard situation today
7

Harmony in Diversity

Africa has over 2,000 languages and 54 nations. The Kingdom belongs to every tribe and tongue. We celebrate difference and refuse every form of tribalism, racism, and division.

Live it · Build a genuine relationship across a cultural line
8

Gracious Courtesy

Treating every human being with dignity and respect — regardless of their status, background, or behavior toward you. The King touched the untouchable. So must we.

Live it · Treat the person society ignores with full dignity today
9

Pursuit of Excellence

Doing your best work as an act of worship. Daniel was ten times better than his peers. The African reformer represents the King — mediocrity is not an option.

Live it · Bring your absolute best to the work in front of you today
10

Steadfast Diligence

Patient, persistent, consistent effort — even when results are slow. The reformation is not built in a moment. It is built day by day, deed by deed, by people who refuse to quit.

Live it · Return to the task you have been avoiding — and finish it
11

Active Justice

Speaking up and acting when others are silent. Justice is not a feeling — it is a deed. To do righteousness and justice is more acceptable to God than sacrifice. Proverbs 21:3.

Live it · Speak up for someone who cannot speak for themselves
12

Faith in Action

Belief that does not stay in the pew. Faith that walks out of the church and into the street, the office, the courtroom, and the community. Faith proved by deeds, not declarations.

Live it · Do one thing this week that only makes sense if God is real

These Values Are Not a Program.
